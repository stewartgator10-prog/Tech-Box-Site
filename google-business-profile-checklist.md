# Google Business Profile Checklist — Tech Box Solutions

Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single biggest lever for showing up in local
search and Google Maps for "[service] near me" and "[service] in Frederick" queries.
It's free. Work through this top to bottom — the whole thing takes an afternoon,
and consistency with the website matters more than anything fancy.

**Your key business facts (keep these identical everywhere):**
- **Name:** Tech Box Solutions
- **City / area served:** Frederick, MD + Frederick County, Maryland
- **Website:** https://techboxsolution.com
- **Email:** stewartgator10@gmail.com  *(consider a branded address later, e.g. hello@techboxsolution.com)*

---

## 1. Create & verify the profile
- [ ] Go to **google.com/business** and sign in with the Google account you want to own this (a dedicated business Gmail is ideal).
- [ ] Search for "Tech Box Solutions" first to make sure a profile doesn't already exist to claim.
- [ ] Create the profile with the exact business name **Tech Box Solutions** (no keywords stuffed into the name — that's against Google's rules).
- [ ] **Set it up as a Service-Area Business (SAB):** when asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?", choose **No** if you work from home / have no storefront. This **hides your street address** and instead shows the areas you serve.
- [ ] Add service areas: **Frederick, MD** and **Frederick County, MD** (add nearby towns you'd travel to — e.g. Urbana, Walkersville, Middletown, Mount Airy).
- [ ] Complete **verification** (postcard, phone, email, or video — Google will offer what's available for your account). *Nothing shows publicly until you're verified — don't skip this.*

## 2. Fill in every field (100% completeness ranks better)
- [ ] **Primary category:** `Website Designer` *(closest match to your core offer)*.
- [ ] **Additional categories:** `Software Company`, `Business to Business Service`, `Marketing Consultant`, `Computer Consultant` (add the ones that fit).
- [ ] **Phone number** — a consistent business number (Google Voice works if you don't want a second line).
- [ ] **Website:** https://techboxsolution.com
- [ ] **Hours** — set your real availability (even "by appointment"). Add special hours for holidays.
- [ ] **Business description (750 chars):** write it naturally with your services + Frederick. Draft below. 👇
- [ ] **Opening date** — when Tech Box Solutions started.

## 3. Services & products
- [ ] Add each **Service** with a short description:
  - Website Design & Building
  - Custom Tools & Software
  - AI Solutions
  - Automation & Workflow
  - Data & Dashboards
  - Systems Integration
- [ ] Use the **Products** section to feature signature work (e.g. "Chip Drop Dispatch", "EDDM Ad Tracker", "Fleet Maintenance Tracker") with a photo and blurb — these double as visual proof.

## 4. Photos & logo (profiles with photos get far more clicks)
- [ ] **Logo** (square).
- [ ] **Cover photo** (landscape, on-brand).
- [ ] 5–10 supporting images: screenshots of tools/sites you've built, your work setup, a headshot. Real beats stock.
- [ ] Re-upload a fresh photo every few weeks — activity signals an active business.

## 5. Reviews — the biggest ranking + trust factor
- [ ] Get your **review short link** (from the GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews").
- [ ] Ask every happy client to leave a review — text them the link the day you finish a project.
- [ ] **Reply to every review**, good or bad, within a day or two. Mention the service and "Frederick" naturally in replies.
- [ ] Aim for your first **5 reviews** quickly — that's the threshold where the star rating starts showing.

## 6. Keep it active (ongoing — 15 min/week)
- [ ] Post a **Google Post** roughly weekly: a finished project, a tip, or a link to a new blog article (e.g. your "Why a Website…" post).
- [ ] Turn on and monitor **messaging** so leads can text you directly.
- [ ] Answer the **Q&A** section — you can even seed it with a few common questions and answer them yourself.
- [ ] Check the **Performance / Insights** tab monthly (searches, calls, direction requests, website clicks).

## 7. Connect it back to the website (NAP consistency)
- [ ] Make sure **Name, Area, Phone, Website** match *exactly* between GBP, your website footer, and any other listing. Google cross-checks this.
- [ ] List the business on a few other directories with the same info (Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, local Frederick/Chamber directories) — consistent citations boost local ranking.
- [ ] Consider joining the **Frederick County Chamber of Commerce** — a link + citation from a local, trusted site is strong for local SEO.
